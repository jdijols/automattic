FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you start. Still stuck? Our team replies within a day.

How does the free plan work?

The free plan is yours forever — one workspace, up to three projects, no card required. Upgrade only when your team outgrows it.

Can I change plans later?

Any time, in both directions. Upgrades apply instantly; downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, prorated automatically.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes — a no-questions 30-day refund on every paid plan. Email us and we'll process it the same day.

Is my data exported if I leave?

Always. Export everything to open formats with one click — no lock-in, no waiting on a support ticket.

How do I get support?

Every plan includes email support with a one-business-day reply. Team and Scale plans add priority routing and a dedicated contact.
